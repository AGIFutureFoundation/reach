      What Reach is doing

        Honesty note. The seeded people and organisations on the stage are fictional, and nothing here sends anywhere real. Anything you add — profile, people, saved drafts — is stored only in this browser, on this device; export or erase it any time from “Make Reach yours”. Copy and Share on a draft move text you choose into your own apps; Reach itself sends nothing. The web demo reads and drives its own staged apps; reading other apps needs the Android accessibility layer on the roadmap, and iOS gets an approval console instead — the design document in the repository says exactly where each claim stops.

        Reach · an AGI Future Foundation build, made at Do Not Disturb: Phone Use Hackathon (Exit 1) · Frontier Tower, 2026-11-13 · Draft-first, human-approved, suppression-respecting — the rails are the product.

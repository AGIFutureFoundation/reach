          Make Reach yours

          Private on this phone. No account, no server — your profile, your people and your history live in this browser's local storage and never leave the device. The one exception is a sponsor key you connect below — and then Reach calls only that provider, directly.

          Connect the sponsor stack

          Reach is built on today's stack — Tavily for live research, ElevenLabs for the voice, LangSmith for the audit trail, and PhoneUse as the local iPhone execution layer. Bring your own keys: keys stay on this phone. Reach calls each provider directly from this browser — nothing routes through us, no key ever leaves the device except to its own provider — and with nothing connected Reach works exactly as it did before this panel existed.

          ElevenLabs — the voice

          Not connected

          Connected, Listen mode and read-aloud speak in a real ElevenLabs voice instead of the browser's. Any failure falls back to the browser voice instantly — nothing ever goes unsaid. Free tier: 10,000 credits a month.

          LangSmith — the audit trail

          Tracing: off

          Connected, every agent action — draft created, approval granted, cap refusal, suppression refusal, send staged — is traced to your LangSmith project, fire-and-forget: a failed trace never blocks or delays Reach. Metadata only; message text and contact details are never traced. Free tier: 5,000 traces a month.

          PhoneUse — the iOS execution layer (local)

          Not detected

          PhoneUse — this event's own open-source stack — runs on your Mac and drives a USB-connected iPhone: live video, OCR screen reading, tap, type, scroll, behind a local API. It is Reach's iOS answer: an approval card you tap here becomes a PhoneUse task on your own Mac and phone — and nothing reaches PhoneUse that Reach didn't get a tap for. No key exists for this row — the test below simply asks the local service whether it's there. From a public HTTPS page like this one, browsers usually block that call, so “not detected” is the normal result here, not an error; run PhoneUse on the Mac beside you and the check is there for the environments that allow it. Our fork: github.com/AGIFutureFoundation/phoneuse.

          What Reach is doing

            Honesty note. The seeded people and organisations on the stage are fictional, and nothing here sends anywhere real. Anything you add — profile, people, saved drafts — is stored only in this browser, on this device; export or erase it any time from “Make Reach yours”. Copy and Share on a draft move text you choose into your own apps; Reach itself sends nothing. If you connect a sponsor-stack key, Reach calls that one provider directly from this browser — research queries to Tavily, the words being read aloud to ElevenLabs, action metadata (never message text) to LangSmith — and the panel says exactly that before you paste anything. With no keys connected, nothing leaves the device at all. The web demo reads and drives its own staged apps; reading other apps needs the Android accessibility layer on the roadmap, and iOS gets an approval console instead — the design document in the repository says exactly where each claim stops.
